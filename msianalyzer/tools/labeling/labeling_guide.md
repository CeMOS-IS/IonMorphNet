# MSI Ion Image Labeling Guide

This guide defines a consistent labeling scheme for ion images in mass spectrometry imaging (MSI). The labels help to distinguish structural and signal-related patterns in ion distributions.

---

## Label: `structured`

**Definition:**  
The entire structure of the sample is clearly visible. Signal is dense, continuous, and fills the region without gaps.  

**Typical appearance:**  
Bright, cohesive ion distribution; sharp anatomical features.

---

## Label: `weak_structured`

**Definition:**  
The entire structure is visible, but with low intensity or thinner signal. There may be gaps or reduced contrast.  

**Typical appearance:**  
Weaker intensity; structure looks faint or washed out.

---

## Label: `localized`

**Definition:**  
Signal is present only in specific localized regions of the sample. These areas are confined and do not represent the full structure.  

**Typical appearance:**  
One or two bright, confined signal spots.

---

## Label: `fragmented`

**Definition:**  
Signal is broken into multiple separate dense clusters. These clusters are distinct but not continuous.  

**Typical appearance:**  
Multiple strong signal regions with no unified shape.

---

## Label: `unstructured`

**Definition:**  
Signal appears random, noisy, or sparse, with no discernible shape or pattern. No anatomical correspondence.  

**Typical appearance:**  
Diffuse or random signal, scattered without clear pattern.

---

## Label: `negative`

**Definition:**  
No relevant signal within the sample region. The image shows only the outside of the sample region continuously.  
Minor noise or fragments inside the sample do not disqualify this label, as long as the outside region shows significantly higher intensity and dominates the image.

**Typical appearance:**  
Sample region is dark, and the area outside the sample is clearly and brightly visible.
